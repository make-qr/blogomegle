A random video chat is a live camera conversation with one stranger at a time. You do not pick the person from a profile grid. A matcher pairs you with someone else who is waiting in a compatible line.

On OmegleChat that line is simple. You choose video or text, then Domestic, International, or Any. Domestic looks in your country first. International looks elsewhere first. Any starts worldwide. If a narrower line is empty, the search can widen. You confirm you are 18 or older, allow the camera and microphone for video, and wait until another adult is ready.

There is no account, no public profile, and no saved transcript of the website chat. The browser keeps a device id so Block can avoid the same pair. The other person can see a country name, not your home address. A live video connection can still reveal a network address to the other browser. That is how WebRTC works. Treat the call as public.

Next ends the chat. The other person is told the stranger disconnected. You are not obligated to explain yourself. Report and Block are different buttons. Report is a note about what happened. Block is a request not to be paired with that device again while the server still remembers it.

OmegleChat is not the old Omegle service and is not affiliated with it. Omegle closed in 2023. This site is a separate product for adults.
