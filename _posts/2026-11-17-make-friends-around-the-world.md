Meeting someone from another country is easy on a random chat. Becoming friends with them is slower. Friendship is repetition plus reliability. One sparkling call is not that.

If the first talk is good, suggest a second with a time. "Same day next week, text only if video is hard" is a plan. Trading apps can wait until you would notice if they did not show up.

Ask about their week, not their nation. People are tired of representing 80 million neighbors. You can still learn. "What did lunch look like?" teaches more than "Tell me about your culture."

Mind the clock. A good hour for you may be the middle of their night. Say your time out loud. Do not treat a delayed reply as a loss of interest if you never agreed on a window.

Keep the early friendship low stakes. Share a song, a recipe, a walk you took. Do not send money, documents, or a spare key to the story of your life. A real friend can wait for those.

OmegleChat can introduce two adults. It cannot maintain the friendship. That part happens because both of you decide to return.
