A good question can be answered without a biography. Use one, then listen. These are starters, not a script you have to finish.

The room. What can you see without turning around? Is it loud or quiet where you are? What did you eat last? Are you at a table or on a sofa?

The day. What was the least important thing you did today? What are you avoiding? What time does your day actually start? Did anything small go right?

Taste. Tea or coffee, and do you finish it? A film you would watch again this month? A meal you can cook without a recipe? A song you skip every time it appears?

Place. What is a smell you associate with home? What do people misunderstand about where you live? Where would you take a friend for an hour, not a weekend? What is closed on Sundays where you are?

People. Who do you text when a plan changes? What do you wish guests would stop doing? When do you prefer silence?

Stop if the other person gives short answers twice. Stop if a question is about a body, a minor, money they did not offer, or an address. Curiosity is not a right to their life.
