People talk to strangers because a stranger has not sorted them yet. A friend already knows the job, the breakup, the opinion you are tired of repeating. A stranger meets the sentence you choose today.

That freedom is pleasant. It is also thin. The stranger cannot comfort you with history they do not have. If you need to be known, a random chat is a rehearsal, not the relationship.

Some people are practicing a language. Some are awake in a quiet house. Some are curious what a person in another time zone had for dinner. Those are decent reasons. Using a stranger to say things you would not say to someone who could find you is a worse reason. The screen is not a mask that deletes consequences. You can still hurt someone, and you can still be recorded.

Enjoy the talk. Then notice whether you feel steadier or more restless when it ends. If every call leaves you more hungry, the format is feeding the feeling instead of answering it. Take a week off and see. A tool you cannot pause is no longer a pastime.
