## What is Omezy?

**Omezy** ([omezy.net](https://omezy.net/)) is a random chat platform in the tradition of classic Omegle:

- **Text chat** — type with strangers, skip anytime
- **Voice chat** — microphone only, no camera required
- **Video chat** — face-to-face matching in the browser
- **No app install** — works on desktop and mobile browsers
- **No signup** for basic guest sessions
- **Region filters** — same country, international, or any region
- **Interest tags** — optional tags to improve match quality
- **Report tools** — flag bad behavior; repeat offenders can be blocked

The idea is simple: open the site, pick a mode, accept the community rules, and meet someone new. When the conversation ends — or gets weird — hit **Next** and move on.

Omezy is aimed at people who miss the old **“talk to strangers online”** flow without downloading random APKs or creating yet another social account.

## How Omezy works (step by step)

1. **Open** [omezy.net](https://omezy.net/) in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
2. **Choose a mode** — text, voice, or video.
3. **Pick a region** — domestic, international, or any.
4. **Add an interest tag** (optional) if you want slightly better matches.
5. **Accept community rules** — most sites require 18+ acknowledgment for video/voice.
6. **Allow mic/camera** only if you chose voice or video.
7. **Say hello** — or skip immediately for a new stranger.

Guest voice and video sessions on Omezy typically include a **time limit** (around 15 minutes in the current version) so sessions stay casual rather than endless. That is a reasonable default for first-time users.

## Safety tips before you start (non-negotiable)

Random chat is only fun when you stay in control. Before any session on Omezy, OmegleChat, or any alternative:

1. **Use HTTPS only** — check the padlock in the address bar.
2. **Never share** your full name, address, phone, school, workplace, or payment info.
3. **Start in text** if you are new — camera off until you trust the flow.
4. **Hit Next / Leave** the second someone pressures you — you owe no one a debate.
5. **Use Report** when content violates rules — it protects the next person too.
6. **Assume 18+** for video/voice — do not use these sites if you are underage.
7. **Read one safety page once** — [OmegleChat safety tips](https://omeglechat.online/safety-tips.html) apply to any stranger chat.

No platform can guarantee every user is honest. **Your judgment is the last line of defense.**

## Red flags on any “Omegle alternative” (including fake Omezy copies)

Scammers love popular search terms. Before you grant camera access:

- ❌ Site pushes a **download** or “Omegle APK”
- ❌ Fake virus popup or full-screen alert
- ❌ Demands **crypto**, gift cards, or “verification fee”
- ❌ No **Next** or **Report** button
- ❌ Plain HTTP (no SSL)
- ❌ URL typo-squatting (`0mezy.net`, `omezy-free-download.com`, etc.)

Stick to the **official domain** you trust. If unsure, close the tab.
