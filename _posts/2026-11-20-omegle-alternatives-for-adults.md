Omegle was a random chat website. It shut down in November 2023. The founder said the cost of lawsuits and abuse on the platform made it impossible to continue. That company is gone. A site that uses the word Omegle in its domain is not automatically that company, and it should say so.

OmegleChat says so here: we are not affiliated with Omegle. The name describes the kind of chat people still look for, which is one stranger at a time in a browser. It is not a claim that we inherited their users, their brand, or their logo.

If you are comparing alternatives, compare behavior. Who is allowed in? What happens when you press Next? Can you report and block? Is there an account? Does video stay in the browser? Those questions matter more than a logo that looks familiar.

Be wary of pages that exist only to repeat "Omegle alternative" and then send you through a chain of pop-ups. A useful page tells you what the product does, who it refuses, and how to leave.
