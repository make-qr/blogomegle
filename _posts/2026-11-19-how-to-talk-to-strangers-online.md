Talking to a stranger online is a skill, and it is ordinary. You already do a smaller version with a cashier or a seatmate. The screen removes the shared room, so you have to offer the room yourself.

Start with where you are in the moment, not who you are on paper. "I'm between tasks" or "I'm walking to the kettle" gives the other person a picture. Then ask one question they can answer without a confession. Food, weather they can see, a sound in the room, a plan for the next hour.

Match their length. If they write one line, do not send a paragraph. If they ask you something, answer it before you ask another. A conversation is a trade.

Do not ask for a name, a photo, or a city in the first minute. Those requests make a cautious person leave, and they make a careless person overshare. You can have a real talk with neither.

If you want to keep going, say so. "This is easy. Want to keep talking about the trip?" If you do not, leave cleanly. Interest is not a debt.
