"No registration" means you do not create an email login to start. On OmegleChat you open the site, pick video or text, confirm your year of birth, and wait for one other adult.

It does not mean the site knows nothing. The browser keeps a device id, your year of birth, and the mode you chose, so the age check and Block can work on that device. Website messages are relayed live and are not kept as a transcript. Your pair can see a country, not your street. Video can expose a network address through the call itself.

Free means you are not paying OmegleChat to be matched. The site shows advertising. An ad is not a reason to click a download or a "verify you are human" pop-up that asks for a password. The real chat stays in the browser tab you opened.

If a page asks you to install a mystery app, send a code, or pay to "unlock" the stranger, close it. That is not how this product works.
