Privacy on a random chat is a set of refusals you can say politely.

Do not give a full name. A first name is already optional. Do not give a phone number, a workplace, a school, or a neighborhood specific enough to walk to. Do not show mail, badges, or a screen with accounts open. Do not read out a verification code. Anyone who asks for a code is trying to enter something that belongs to you.

Do not send a photo to prove you are real. The live camera is already a photo stream. An extra picture is a file you cannot get back. Do not accept a file you did not ask for.

Do not announce that you live alone, that you are traveling, or what time the house is empty. Ordinary talk does not need that.

Use the browser you trust, and close the tab when you are finished. The website does not keep a transcript of the chat. Your device may still keep the page in its history. That part is yours to clear.

If something felt wrong, Report it. If you simply do not want that person again, Block. Then do something in your own room that is not the chat. The boundary works better when the next hour is yours.
