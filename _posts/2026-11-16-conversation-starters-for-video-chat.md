On video, silence feels longer than it is. Have three openers you can say without performing.

"I just sat down. What are you in the middle of?"
"My camera is pointing at a messy shelf. What is behind you that you are willing to show?"
"I have about fifteen minutes. Is that all right?"
"Tell me one thing you can hear."
"I am drinking something too hot. What is in your cup?"
"What is the last small errand you finished?"
"I do not want a debate. What made you open this tab?"
"Show me an object you use every day, if you want."
"What are you looking forward to this week that is not work?"
"If this call ended in two minutes, what would you still want to say?"

Say one. Then wait. Filling the pause with a second question teaches the other person they do not have to answer the first.

If they flirt and you do not want that, say "I'd rather keep this ordinary." If the call turns sexual and you are not interested, leave. You do not owe a softer version.
