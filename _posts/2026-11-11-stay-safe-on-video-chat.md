OmegleChat is for adults 18 and over. Do not use it if you are younger, and do not keep a conversation going if you believe the other person is a minor. End it and use Report. Do not investigate.

Before you start, look at the frame. No documents, no school names, no mail, no children, no workplace badges. Use a background that could be anyone's wall.

During the call, do not share your full name, address, workplace, daily route, or a code from your phone. Do not move the chat to a promise of money. A person who needs your bank details in the first meeting does not need a second meeting.

Leave when you want to. Next disconnects you. You may also say one sentence and go. Report is the note about what happened. Block asks the server not to pair you with that device again while it still remembers the pair. They are separate. You can use one or both. There is not a moderator sitting in the call.

Video can reveal a network address to the other browser. Country is what the product shows on purpose. Do not add your street to be helpful.

The written rules are on the main site's safety page. This article is the short version you can remember with the camera already on.
