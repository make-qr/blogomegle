A kind joke has a target you are allowed to tease: the situation, the weather, your own mistake. It does not need a person in the room to lose.

1. I set three alarms so I could ignore them in a sequence.
2. My houseplant is not dying. It is method acting.
3. I opened the fridge for inspiration and it suggested cheese, which I respected.
4. I told myself I would read one chapter. The bookmark and I are no longer speaking.
5. The group chat made a plan. I replied "love this" and then did not go, which is a full personality.
6. I bought the healthy snack and the emergency chocolate. Only one of them has a future.
7. I said I would leave at nine. My coat believed me. I did not.
8. I asked the map for a shortcut and it sent me past the place I was trying to avoid, which felt personal.

If you tell these, leave space after the line. A joke that needs an explanation was not ready. If nobody laughs, you can say "that one stays in the notebook" and move on. Do not repeat it louder.

Do not swap in a friend's body, accent, job, or dating life to make the line sharper. The laugh you get that way is expensive. The person remembers the cost longer than the table remembers the joke.

At home, the jokes that last are the ones you both can retell to a guest without anyone having to smile through it. If you would not say it with their parent in the kitchen, it is not kind yet.
