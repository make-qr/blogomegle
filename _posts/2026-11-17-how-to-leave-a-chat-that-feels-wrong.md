A bad chat rarely starts with a threat. It starts with a question you did not agree to, a joke that makes you smaller, or a person who will not accept a short answer.

You can leave. On OmegleChat the Next button ends the match. You do not have to explain the button. If you want one sentence first, use one of these and then go:

"I'm done. Take care."
"No. I'm leaving."
"That question is a no. Goodbye."

Do not stay to teach them manners. A stranger who ignored the first no will use your explanation as a new handle. The lesson is the closed window.

Do not send a photo to "prove" you are real, old enough, or not rude. A picture is data. Once it leaves your phone you do not control it. A voice note is the same kind of decision. If you would not hand it to someone on a bus, do not hand it to a username.

Do not give a workplace, a school, a neighborhood, or a daily route. "A city" is already more than a first chat needs. If they guess and push, that push is the reason to leave, not a reason to correct them.

If the person is a minor, or is sexual toward anyone who might be a minor, end the chat and use Report. Do not keep talking to investigate. Block is separate from Report on this site: Block stops a rematch for as long as the server remembers it, and Report is the note you leave about what happened. Use the one you mean. You can use both.

After you leave, do not reopen the tab to check whether they were "actually fine." The feeling you had was enough. Drink water, message a friend who knows your name, or close the laptop. You did not abandon a relationship. You ended a conversation that had not earned more of you.

If you want the written rules, they live on the main site at the safety page. This essay is the short version: one sentence, then Next.
