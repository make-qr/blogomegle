Use a current browser on a phone or a computer. Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and the mobile browser are enough. You do not install an app for the OmegleChat website.

Sit where you can be overheard only by people you accept in the room. A random stranger should not meet your roommate by accident, and a child should not be in the frame. This product is for adults 18 and over.

Choose Video Chat, then a region. Allow the camera and microphone when the browser asks. If you deny the camera, the video session cannot start. Text chat does not need either permission.

When someone appears, say hello and one ordinary fact. "I'm having tea. What is in front of you?" is enough. Do not open with a request for a photo, a city, or a social account. You can have a good conversation without any of those.

If the call feels wrong, press Next. You can also say "I'm done" and then leave. Do not stay to win an argument with a stranger.
