Loneliness is the gap between the contact you have and the contact you can count on. A day full of notifications can still have that gap. The messages arrive, and none of them would change if you went quiet.

Digital life makes the gap easier to see. You can watch other people together. You can also reach a stranger in a minute, which is new and not the same as being accompanied. A stranger can be kind for ten minutes. They cannot be the person who knows your Tuesday.

If you are lonely, start with something that has a body. Sleep, a meal at a table, a walk, one message to someone who already has your name. Then, if you want, a conversation with a stranger. In that order the stranger is company. In the other order the stranger is a substitute you will have to replace again tonight.

This is not a diagnosis and it is not therapy. If loneliness is heavy for weeks, talk to someone who can stay: a friend, a clinician, a local service. A video chat can sit beside that. It should not be the only plan.
