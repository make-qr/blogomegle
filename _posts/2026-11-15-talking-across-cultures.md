A cultural difference is not a quiz you can win. It is a habit you do not share yet: how long a pause lasts, whether a direct no is polite, whether lunch is a meeting or a break.

On a video chat you will misread something. A delayed smile can be lag. A short answer can be caution, not coldness. Say what you noticed and ask. "I thought that pause meant you were done. Were you?" is better than a theory about their country.

Do not open with politics, religion, or a joke about an accent. You do not know what that joke cost them last week. If they bring up home, follow their detail. If they do not, talk about the day you are both having.

Humor travels badly when it depends on an insult. It travels well when it is about your own mistake: the alarm you ignored, the plant you underwatered, the map that sent you in a circle.

Learn one concrete thing and remember it next time. That is culture, practiced. A list of "facts about countries" is not. If you only have one conversation, leave them with the sense that you were talking to them, not to a passport.
